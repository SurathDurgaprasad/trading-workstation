# Phase 11 — Stop-Condition Assessment

Per the mission's own four stop conditions, assessed honestly against Phase 1–10's actual findings.

## Condition 1: a research dependency genuinely requires external data/access that is unavailable

**Met, for both currently-strongest families, in different ways.**
- F_MEANREV's remaining open question (how much of its positive validation/OOS result depends on
  current-F&O-eligibility survivorship bias) requires a genuine point-in-time historical universe.
  Phase 2 confirmed the authoritative source exists (NSE's own F&O bhavcopy archive) but full
  reconstruction is a substantial, separately-scoped engineering task (bulk multi-year retrieval,
  historical format handling, ToS/rate-limit verification) — not something to build inside this same
  autonomous research session without dedicated scoping.
- F_CONTEXT's remaining open question (is the market-divergence effect's era-dependent sign flip
  explainable by a specific regime variable, or is it simply unstable) requires a NEW, pre-registered
  hypothesis with its own regime-conditioning variable — not more of the same unconditioned test on
  more data, which is exactly what H_CONTEXT_MARKET_005 already ran.

## Condition 2 (its stated positive form does not apply; its natural counterpart does)

No meaningful edge was demonstrated that requires a human promotion decision. **The mission's own
alternative objective has instead been met**: "strong evidence that the currently available research
framework cannot demonstrate one." Phase 10 showed this is not a single weak result but a convergent
pattern across 56 hypotheses / 17 independent research families, with the two most rigorously
-tested candidates each failing for a different, non-overlapping reason. Deciding whether to fund
the two Condition-1 follow-on engineering efforts (point-in-time universe; regime-conditioned
F_CONTEXT re-test) is itself a resource-allocation decision for a human, not something this session
should decide unilaterally by simply starting one.

## Condition 3: the current research avenue is exhausted

**Met.** F_MEANREV: signal-strength selection ruled out (H_MEANREV_012), clustering ruled out
(H_MEANREV_013), multiple-testing sensitivity now quantified (Phase 8) — no further portfolio
-construction variant is justified without first resolving the universe dependency (explicitly not
this session's scope, per Phase 2's own decision). F_CONTEXT: cost/liquidity/sector/concentration
robustness already exhaustively checked (H_CONTEXT_MARKET_004); the 10-year extension
(H_CONTEXT_MARKET_005) already surfaced the disqualifying finding — further slicing of the same
unconditioned test would not add information, per that entry's own recorded conclusion. All other
families are either already closed (F_GAP, F_XSECT) or too thin to warrant further audit-only
scrutiny without new data collection.

## Condition 4: hard safety/data-integrity boundary

**Not met, and not the reason for stopping here** — no safety or data-integrity violation was found
in Phases 1–3 (Phase 3's own conclusion: "Genuine defects found: NONE"). Stopping here is a research
-conclusion decision (Conditions 1–3), not a safety-triggered halt.

## Overall determination

**Stop autonomous hypothesis-generation here.** Conditions 1 and 3 are both independently satisfied;
Condition 2's spirit (a result significant enough to need a human decision) is satisfied by its
negative-finding counterpart, which the mission's own stated objective explicitly anticipated as a
valid, complete outcome. Starting a new, ungrounded H57 now — without either engineering dependency
resolved — would repeat exactly the "maximize hypotheses tested" pattern the mission's own opening
sentence explicitly said NOT to do. The correct next action is a full, honest final report to the
user, not another experiment.

Phase 12 (a live pilot / promotion path) does not apply — no candidate hypothesis reached a
promotable state.
