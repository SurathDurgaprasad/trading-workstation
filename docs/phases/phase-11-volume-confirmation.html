Volume signal confirmation study — Phase 11

The falsification succeeded: volume_ratio does not confirm

A dedicated attempt to confirm or falsify Phase 10's single B-classified feature (volume_ratio) as a real, causal, cost-surviving trading signal — exactly 3 pre-registered candidates, standalone and as a filter over the frozen TrendMomentumBaseline. One candidate showed a real-looking pooled out-of-sample improvement; per-symbol inspection showed it was concentrated in 2-3 symbols' large swings, not a broad effect, and failed the cross-symbol consistency bar. H0 stands: no candidate is confirmed.

3 candidates × 2 forms (standalone + filtered) = 6 tested 0 changes to strategy/baseline.py, risk/, paper/, backtesting/ 288/289 tests passing (1 pre-existing, unrelated) Reproducibility: byte-identical Classification: C (5 of 6), B (1 of 6, insufficient sample)
Bottom line

H0 not rejected. volume_ratio does not contain enough stable, cross-symbol-consistent, cost-surviving predictive information to support a confirmed trading rule — neither standalone nor as a filter over the frozen strategy. Every full-period pooled net PnL remains negative at baseline cost. The two "high-volume" filtered candidates (A and its extreme-volume cousin C) showed a genuinely positive pooled out-of-sample number (+$1,462 and +$1,264 respectively, vs. control's −$1,295) — but per-symbol inspection shows this was driven almost entirely by large swings in 2-3 symbols (AAPL and XOM alone account for more than the entire pooled improvement), with only 5-6 of 12 symbols actually positive or beating control in that window. That fails the cross-symbol consistency bar this study set for itself before looking at any result.

The one candidate with a small-sample OOS number too extreme to trust at face value (filtered low-volume, +0.87R on 5 OOS trades) is classified B — inconclusive due to insufficient sample, exactly the outcome spec's own methodology anticipates for a thin cut, not upgraded because the sign happened to be positive.

2Exact volume_ratio definition (frozen, unchanged)

volume_ratio = volume / rolling_mean(volume, window=20, min_periods=20)

Defined in market/indicators.py::compute_volume_ratio_series, computed once as part of every symbol's standard compute_indicator_series() output. This phase imports and uses this exact column — no OBV, VWAP, Chaikin Money Flow, volume RSI, or any other transformation was implemented or considered.

4Rationale and direction

Direction was read directly from Phase 10's own evidence (§1): higher volume_ratio associated with higher forward 5-day returns, so every candidate is LONG-biased. Candidate A is the direct reading of the dominant Q3-Q5-vs-Q1-Q2 pattern. Candidate B is a falsification arm, motivated by Phase 10's own finding that the bottom quintile (Q1, +0.08%) was NOT the worst bucket — Q2 was — so testing whether low volume also carries positive information (or doesn't) is informative either way. Candidate C tests whether "unusual" volume in either direction (matching the non-monotonic Q2-dip shape) is more informative than "normal" volume. Each was tested BOTH standalone (research/volume_signal.py::VolumeSignalStrategy, ignoring SMA/RSI/MACD entirely) and as a filter over the frozen TrendMomentumBaseline (via strategy.regime_filters.FilteredStrategy, reused unchanged from Phase 9) — spec's explicit two-question split: does the feature have standalone value, and does it add value to the existing strategy.

Exit structure reuses TrendMomentumBaseline's own stop (ATR14×1.5) and target (2R) constants exactly — chosen specifically to isolate the entry signal's value from exit design, and to avoid introducing any new execution primitive. No maximum holding period is imposed (positions exit only via stop/target/end-of-data, identical to every existing strategy).

5Causal implementation

research/volume_signal.py (new) implements VolumeSignalStrategy (standalone) and make_volume_predicate (for FilteredStrategy), both reading only indicator_series.iloc[index] at generation time — no rolling computation of their own beyond the already-frozen (p20, p80) pair. 17 new tests (tests/test_volume_signal.py) verify: each candidate's exact directional logic against known values; truncated-history and mutate-the-future causality controls; and — specifically re-verified for a volume-based predicate — the suppress-only guarantee FilteredStrategy already proved in Phase 9.

Every standalone candidate shows a large GROSS profit (up to +18,434 for standalone A) that evaporates and reverses hard the moment baseline costs are applied — the higher trade counts these standalone candidates generate (up to 491 trades, more than double control's 218) multiply the fixed per-fill brokerage, exactly the mechanism spec's cost-survival section warns about. 11Incremental value over TrendMomentumBaseline

Filtered A and filtered C reduce the pooled full-period LOSS relative to control (§6) — a real, reproducible, but modest effect (control −7,547 → filtered A −2,049 / filtered C −4,125), achieved by filtering control's own signal set down (218 → 190/193 trades) rather than adding new ones. This is a genuine, if small, incremental effect on loss magnitude. It does NOT translate into incremental value on profitability, cross-symbol breadth, or positive-expectancy count (§6-7) — the filter makes the existing strategy's losses smaller without making it a winning strategy or improving its consistency across the universe.

13Out-of-sample — the decisive test

A pooled positive that doesn't survive per-symbol inspection

Filtered A and filtered C both show real pooled OOS improvement over control. Per-symbol OOS breakdown tells a different story: filtered A beats control in only 6/12 symbols and is net-positive in only 5/12 (filtered C: 7/12 and 5/12). The pooled +$1,462 (filtered A) is smaller than AAPL's own OOS swing alone (control −$626 → filtered A +$1,562, a +$2,188 change) — meaning the pooled number is driven by a small number of symbols' large moves, not a broad effect, with per-symbol OOS trade counts as thin as 1-8 trades each. This is exactly the concentration risk spec's cross-symbol section warns against, and it fails the "cross-symbol reasonably consistent" bar for classification A regardless of the pooled sign.

Filtered B's OOS result (+$2,133, R=+0.868) rests on 5 trades total, pooled across all 12 symbols — explicitly too small a sample to interpret one way or the other, per spec's own stated rule ("if OOS trade count is too small, classify as inconclusive"). It is not treated as evidence of anything, positive or negative.

14Statistical uncertainty

Every standalone-feature OOS bootstrap CI (§10) includes zero. No backtested variant's improvement over control was tested with a formal significance test beyond the per-symbol/cross-symbol consistency checks in §7/§13, because the concentration finding there is already dispositive — a result driven by 2-3 symbols' large moves does not need a p-value to be judged not-yet-confirmed; the cross-symbol breadth criterion itself, pre-specified before this study ran, already fails.

15Economic significance

Gross vs. net: standalone A's zero-cost pooled result (+18,434) collapses to −6,304 once baseline costs apply — a swing of over $24,700 attributable entirely to transaction costs on 491 trades (≈$50/trade round-trip average). Filtered A's smaller trade count (190) shows a smaller but still decisive gross-to-net collapse (+7,377 → −2,049, ≈$49/trade). The per-trade cost margin (~$49-50 round-trip) is not small relative to this signal's per-trade edge at any tested variant — none of the six candidates demonstrates a gross edge per trade large enough to comfortably clear this margin at scale.

22What this does NOT prove

  • This does not prove volume_ratio is pure noise. The standalone statistical evidence (§10) shows a positive, if weak and non-significant, OOS relationship — the falsification here is specifically about whether it survives conversion into a confirmed, cross-symbol-consistent, cost-surviving TRADING RULE with these particular thresholds and exit structure, not about the raw feature's information content in the abstract.
  • This does not prove a different threshold, exit structure, or holding period would fail the same way. Exactly 3 pre-registered rules with one frozen exit structure were tested, deliberately not a search — spec's explicit anti-overfitting boundary.
  • This does not prove filtered A/C's OOS improvement was pure chance. It shows the improvement is concentrated and thin enough that it cannot be distinguished from a small-sample, few-symbol artifact with the evidence gathered here — a different, more cautious claim than "proven to be noise."
  • This does not test volume_ratio in combination with any other feature — explicitly out of scope, per spec's "no combinatorial search."

23Recommendation for Phase 12

Do not carry any of the 6 candidates forward for deployment or further tuning. Per spec's most important rule, a losing/inconclusive result is reported as such, not rescued by adjusting thresholds, exits, or position sizing after the fact — none of that was done here. The volume_ratio hypothesis, having now been both discovered (Phase 10) and falsification-tested (Phase 11) through this platform's full validation pipeline, should be considered closed absent a genuinely new hypothesis about why or how it might work differently (a different frozen threshold methodology, a different exit structure) — which would itself need to be a fresh, separately pre-registered study, not a continuation of this one. The platform's proven engineering correctness — reused unmodified across four consecutive research phases (8, 9, 10, 11) without a single change to strategy/, risk/, paper/, or backtesting/ — remains this research program's most durable, validated result.
